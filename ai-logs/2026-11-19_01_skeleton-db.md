  4. What I did and why

  - Pool: pkg/db creates one pgx pool on first use and keeps it for the life of
    the process. A warm Vercel instance then reuses its connections. It uses at
    most 4 connections, closes idle ones after 30 s, and waits at most 5 s for a
    new connection. pgx defaults are otherwise untouched, so they work with
    Neon's PgBouncer.
  - Health handler: it depends on a small DBPinger interface, so tests use a
    fake instead of a real database. It pings with a 5 s timeout. It returns 200
    with the latency on success, and 503 with a generic body on failure. The
    real error goes to the server log only.
  - Router: NewRouter(Deps{DB: ...}) takes its dependencies as a parameter, and
    both api/index.go and cmd/local build the same ones.
  - Frontend client: apiFetch parses JSON, throws a typed ApiError on non-2xx,
    and times out after 15 s.
  - Slow signal: the useApi hook sets slow once a request has been running for
    1.5 s. I made it a hook because slow is timer-driven UI state, and that
    keeps apiFetch a plain function.
  - App.tsx: it tells "API reachable, DB down" apart from "API failed" using the
    503 body, which ApiError keeps.
